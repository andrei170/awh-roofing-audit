Two Jobs At Once · AWH Roofing Ltd

You Are Not Short Of Work Or Skill. You Are Short Of Concurrency.

Prepared for Alex Hickin · 3 October 2026, after our call · AG1 Consulting
This Page Replaced The One That Was Here
We had prepared the wrong argument and you corrected it

Before the call this page argued that you should chase bigger jobs and push your average value up. You never said that was the problem, and it is not.

What you actually described is this:

“You might do a few days on one job, disappear from there for a little bit. So obviously the productivity goes down, because you started one job, you're leaving that to start another one. So it means you're not generating money on two jobs now, you're just generating money on one.”

And then the part that actually hurts:

“You've got materials on the job, but then obviously you don't generally get paid until the job's finished. So that can cause a bit of an issue.”

⭐⭐ That is not a revenue problem. It is a cash conversion problem. Money is going out on materials for two jobs while only one of them is moving toward the invoice that pays for both.

Why A Second Pair Of Hands Is The Lever
⭐ Your own framing, which is better than ours was

You said you want a more efficient workforce, not a bigger one. The maths behind that is simple and it is worth seeing written down.

  • Three skilled men on one roof is three skilled men doing one job.
  • Three skilled men on three roofs, each with a labourer, is three jobs.
  • You are not adding skill. You are not adding capability. You are removing the thing that forces your skilled men to stand next to each other.

⭐ And you already have the skilled men and the work. That is what makes this unusual. Most businesses we look at are short of one or the other. You are short of neither, which means the cheapest input in the business is the one that unlocks it.

What It Is Actually Costing, In Your Numbers

Blank on purpose. We are not guessing at your figures, and you told us plainly that generic was not good enough. ⭐ Five minutes with these on Saturday and we would know whether this is worth doing at all.

D and E together are the real number. ⭐ Materials paid for, sat on a roof, earning nothing, while the invoice that covers them waits for a job you cannot get back to. F is the one that will probably surprise you, because a job you turned down six months ago does not feel like a cost.
The Honest Limits Of This
⛔ Three things a labourer does not fix
  • A labourer does not double your output. He removes a constraint on running jobs in parallel. If your real constraint turns out to be your own time — quoting, surveying, client calls, and being on the tools half the week — then the second man helps less than you think and we would rather find that out on Saturday.
  • Concurrency has its own cost. Three jobs running means three sets of materials ordered, three clients ringing, three sites to visit. ⚠️ You are the person all of that lands on, and you are already on a roof half the week.
  • It does not fix the cash gap, it shortens it. You still do not get paid until a job finishes. Running two to completion instead of one half-finished brings the invoice forward; it does not change the terms.

⛔ And the one that is not a marketing problem at all: you told us you do not employ anybody because the trade runs on subcontract. An apprentice, properly speaking, is somebody being trained and supervised, which usually means employing them. That is a question for your accountant rather than for us, and it may change what we are advertising for. We would rather raise it now than build a campaign around an arrangement that does not work.

What This Means For What We Build
  • 1. The hiring page is the priority, and it is aimed at young lads. Not experienced roofers. That page has been rewritten.
  • 2. It goes on your own domain. awhroofing.co.uk/hiring, and you keep it when any campaign stops.
  • 3. Measurement goes on the site first. Otherwise neither of us can tell whether the page or the campaign did anything.
  • 4. The job write-ups and the old testimonials come back. ⭐ They work on customers and on a lad deciding whether this is a proper firm.
  • 5. The stale Google listings get cleaned up. You raised it, and somebody checking you out should find one business, not three and your mum's old address.
  • 6. The repair enquiries get turned away on the site, as you agreed. Costs you work on purpose.

Three skilled men, three roofs, three labourers. You already have the men and the work.

What you are missing is the cheapest part of it.
