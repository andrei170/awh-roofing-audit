Six Specialist Roofers Around You. Not One Is Visibly Hiring.

Prepared for Alex Hickin · read 9 October 2026 · AG1 Consulting
Who A Client Compares You With
How a specified roof or a leadwork job gets bought

A client or a main contractor rarely rings the first roofer they find. They draw up a shortlist, then they look at each firm's website. That is where the shortlist gets cut.

We read the own websites of six specialist roofers around you on 9 October. Here is what each one shows.

Where You Stand
Where you are stronger
  • Four named projects on your site: Bray Refurbishment, Hale End Road, Maldon Lane and Middleway. Only Ashe names projects at all
  • Dedicated pages for Protan, Res-Tec and RubberBond. You told us people already find you by searching for those installers
  • The Lead Sheet Association, which you told us you belong to, against firms that only say "lead specialists"
Where you are behind
  • CLS, in your own town, shows NFRC and CompetentRoofer. You told us your CompetentRoofer application is still open. Worth finishing, because it is the first thing a commercial client checks
  • Ashe shows CHAS and Constructionline Gold, which is what gets a firm onto school and council work
And the hiring angle

None of these six is visibly recruiting. The only careers page, Ashe's, has no vacancies and asks for a CV.

⭐⭐ A hiring page on your own site, aimed at young lads and asking for no CV, would make you the only specialist roofer around you that a school leaver can actually find recruiting.
