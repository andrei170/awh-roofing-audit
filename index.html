Most people we audit want more work. You said you cannot staff the work you already have.

That changes what is worth buying, so we have written this the other way round — what the site does to the people you want to hire, and to the buyers of a £20,000 to £100,000 job who are checking you out before they ring.

There is one thing we would fix regardless, and it is not a marketing item. It is the reason your firm is hard to find at all.

The good half is genuinely good, and it is the half most roofers do not have

Named projects and named manufacturer systems. Protan, Res-Tec and RubberBond are specified commercial systems, and publishing them tells a specifier you work at that level.

That is the raw material for both problems below — it is what convinces a good roofer you are worth working for, and what convinces a £100,000 client you are worth ringing.

Finding 1 — There Is No Measurement On This Website At All
EVERY TAG WE LOOKED FOR, ON THE HOMEPAGE Google Analytics 4 not found Google Tag Manager not found Google Ads conversion not found Universal Analytics not found gtag() not found Meta pixel not found Hotjar / Clarity not found Nothing. Not one tag of any kind.
This is consistent with what you told Elijah, and it is not a criticism

You said you run no online marketing and work on referrals. So there has been nothing to measure and nobody has needed to. That is a reasonable position for a business that is full.

We are flagging it because it means nobody can answer a simple question: how many people look you up after a referral, and what do they do next? On £20,000 to £100,000 jobs, a handful of people quietly deciding against you after checking the website is a lot of money.

It is a one-off install and it costs nothing monthly. We would put it in whatever else you decide, because every other question depends on it.

Finding 2 — Your Website Does Not Say Where You Are
STRUCTURED DATA SERVED ON awhroofing.co.uk Types found: WebPage, WebSite, BreadcrumbList, SearchAction LocalBusiness / RoofingContractor NOT PRESENT streetAddress NOT PRESENT addressLocality (town) NOT PRESENT postalCode NOT PRESENT areaServed NOT PRESENT Published contact route: one mobile number, 07889 140051
Two separate problems come out of this

The first is that local search has very little to work with. Search engines place a trade business using exactly the markup that is missing here. A roofer with no published town is hard to return for “flat roofing near me” anywhere in particular.

The second is trust, and it matters more at your job sizes. Somebody about to spend eighty thousand pounds looks for an address and a landline. What they find is a mobile number and no location.

We are not saying a mobile is wrong — plenty of good firms run on one. We are saying that on a site with no address either, the two absences add up to something you would not want them to add up to.

This is the cheapest fix in the document

Adding the business address, trading area and correct markup is an afternoon, and it is the single item most likely to change whether you appear at all for somebody searching without a referral.

Finding 3 — Nothing On This Site Is Written For The People You Actually Need
WHAT YOU TOLD ELIJAH "doesn't have enough staffing to be able to handle the current jobs he has or even potentially get more" "Due to the lack of quality the problem has never been the quantity" WHAT THE WEBSITE OFFERS A ROOFER LOOKING FOR WORK Careers or vacancies page none Anything about the team none found Pay, tickets or progression none How to apply nothing
If the constraint is people, the website is aimed at the wrong audience entirely

Every word on it is addressed to a customer, and you have told us customers are not the shortage. A skilled roofer deciding where to work looks the firm up in exactly the same way a client does, and finds nothing addressed to them.

You already have the strongest recruitment material a roofing firm can have and it is sitting on the wrong pages. Named refurbishment projects and specified systems tell a good roofer this is proper work, not chasing domestic repairs.

What we would build instead of a lead campaign
  • A page written for roofers, not customers. The kind of work, the systems you install, what you pay, what tickets you want, and who to ring. Most roofing firms have nothing like it.
  • The project work reframed as evidence of the job quality — the same four projects serve both audiences without writing them twice.
  • Measurement, so you can see whether anybody reads it. A recruitment page nobody visits is worth knowing about quickly.

None of this brings you work you cannot staff, which is the point.

Finding 4 — 692 Words Under One Heading
HOMEPAGE STRUCTURE Words 692 H1 1 "AWH Roofing Ltd" H2 1 "Trusted Roofing Systems From Leading UK Manufacturers" /contact 154 words
The content is reasonable. It just has no shape.

Seven hundred words under a single subheading is a wall. Nobody scanning it finds the leadwork, the flat roofing systems or the project list, because there is nothing to scan by.

Your H1 is your company name, which spends the most valuable line on the page telling people something they already knew from the logo. What you do and where you do it would work harder there.

What We Would Do, In Order
  • Publish your address and add proper business markup. Cheapest item here and the one that decides whether you turn up for anybody who has not been referred.
  • Install measurement. One-off, no monthly, and nothing after this can be judged without it.
  • Build the recruitment page. If people are the constraint, this is the only item on the list pointed at the actual problem.
  • Give the homepage headings and a better H1. An afternoon's work on content you already have.
  • Do not buy lead generation yet. You told us quantity has never been the problem. We are not going to sell you the opposite of what you said.
The honest question for Saturday

If you could hire three good roofers tomorrow, would the work be there to give them? If yes, the recruitment page is the whole job. If no, then the order changes and we should talk about both.

Named commercial projects, specified systems, and a website that does not say what town you are in.

Fix the second one and the first starts working for you.
