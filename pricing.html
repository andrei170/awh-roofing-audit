What The £1,500 Includes
⭐ All of this, inside the figure above

The hiring page

  • Written and built for young lads who want to learn the trade, not experienced roofers.
  • On your own domain at awhroofing.co.uk/hiring, so it is found under your name.
  • The five sections you saw: the work, who you want, what they get, straight answers, apply.
  • Says what the job is not, so the wrong lads rule themselves out before they ring you.
  • An application form, so twenty-five enquiries do not arrive as twenty-five calls while you are on a roof.
  • Nothing on your existing site is replaced, rebuilt or scrapped.

The campaign

  • Built, targeted by age and area, written and set live.
  • Run and watched for the month you are hiring, then switched off.
  • You see what every applicant cost and where they came from.

And the groundwork, at no extra cost

  • ⭐ Tracking on your website — analytics, tag manager, and conversion tracking on your existing enquiry form and your phone number. You currently have none of this, so for the first time you will be able to see which pages bring work in.
  • ⭐ Local business details in your site's code — address, postcode, hours, coordinates. Absent at the moment, and it is what search engines read for “roofer near me”.
  • ⭐ Sitemap fixed. Yours declares two pages when you have around seventeen. Your Res-Tec and Protan pages are already bringing you work and nothing is pointing Google at them.

⚠️ Those three are included because they are cheap for us and because without the first one neither of us can tell whether any of this worked. They are worth having even if you never run a campaign.

  • Recruiting. ⛔ We are a marketing company. We will not interview anybody, assess anybody, check a reference, or tell you who to take on. We cannot guarantee a hire, a number of applicants, or that anybody who applies will be any good. Deciding stays entirely with you.
  • Employment advice. See the note at the bottom of this page. That one is for your accountant.
If You Need To Hire Again Later
You asked what happens after the month, and the answer is nothing disappears
  • The page stays up. It is yours, on your domain, permanently.
  • Switching the campaign back on costs less than the first time, because the page, the tracking and the targeting already exist. We will quote it when you need it rather than put a number on something that might be two years away.
  • You can also just leave the page up and do nothing. It will still be there when somebody searches, and it costs you nothing to keep.
Worth Setting Against One Number You Already Have

We are not going to tell you what this is worth, because you know and we do not. But you said this on the call:

“I get approached for work and I'm too busy and I can't do it, because I can't split the workers up.”

⭐ So the only comparison that matters is this: how much was one of those jobs worth?

Not a year of them. One. If the answer is a number with four figures in it and a comma, then £1,500 once is a straightforward decision and you do not need us to dress it up. If it is not, tell us on Saturday and we will say so.

⛔ We are not promising you will hire anybody. We are saying the cost of finding out is small against the thing it is trying to fix.

🔴 One Thing To Settle Before You Pay Us Anything
🔴 It affects what we advertise, so it comes first

You told us you employ nobody:

“All of them, really. I don't employ it. I'm just not established enough to. Roofing is one of those sort of, it's a subcontracting industry.”

⚠️ That works for a skilled man with his own van, tools and insurance. It is less straightforward for a nineteen-year-old being trained and supervised all day, who would normally need to be employed rather than subcontracted.

⛔ We are not advising you on that and you should not take advice on it from us. But the answer decides whether we are advertising for a subcontract labourer, an employed trainee, or a college placement, and those are three different adverts.

⭐ A five minute conversation with your accountant before Saturday and we will write to whichever answer you get. We would rather raise it now than build you something that does not fit how you work.
