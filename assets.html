Three Documents, And Not One Of Them Sells You More Enquiries

Prepared for Alex Hickin · Saturday 3 October, 9am · AG1 Consulting
Why This Pack Looks Like This
You said the problem has never been quantity. We took that at face value

If you cannot staff the work you already hold, more enquiries makes your week worse rather than better. So nothing in this pack is a lead generation pitch, and there is no pricing in any of it.

⭐ Instead it deals with the two things that genuinely are in the way: which end of your own £20,000–£100,000 range the work arrives at, and finding people.

Everything In One Place
  • Pre-Call Audit — start here. What your site does well, six problems in order of severity, and seven questions. ⭐ Includes a correction: we were told you have no online presence, and that is not true.
  • Job Value — the arithmetic of the same crew doing the same number of jobs at a different end of your range, and the honest objection to it. This is the main conversation.
  • Hiring — what recruitment advertising can and cannot do, why a job advert has probably not worked, and the three things that decide it which are not marketing.
  • Recruitment Concept Page — a page written for roofers rather than customers. ⚠️ A design proposal, not a live site and not a real job advert.
The Short Version
⭐ What is already good — do not change it

Named projects — Bray, Hale End Road, Maldon Lane, Middleway. Named systems — Protan, Res-Tec, RubberBond. Separate service pages, a working enquiry form, and a stated service area across Essex, Hertfordshire and London.

⭐⭐ And the thing you undersell: a traditional apprenticeship, Lead Sheet Association studies, sheet metal in New Zealand and zinc in Belgium. That is heritage and architectural roofing, which is the top of your range — and your homepage leads with “all aspects of roof replacement and repair”.

🔴 1. There is no measurement of any kind — cheapest fix, goes first

No analytics, no tag manager, no pixel, nothing. You have four project write-ups and no way to know whether a single person has read one.

🔴 2. No trade accreditation appears anywhere — and for leadwork that matters

No NFRC, CompetentRoofer, CHAS, Constructionline or TrustMark. Most pointedly, no Lead Contractors Association. ⭐ If you hold any of these and they are simply not on the site, that is a ten minute fix worth more than a month of advertising.

🔴 3. Your site never says where you are, where it counts — an hour of work

The service area is in your page text, which is good. But there is no local business markup, no postal address, no postcode, no opening hours and no coordinates anywhere in the code — which is what search engines actually read for “roofer near me”.

⚠️ 4. Nothing on the site is aimed at the people you need — the real constraint

No careers page, no team page, no photographs of the crew, nothing a roofer would read. ⭐ And you hold the best recruitment material a roofing firm could have. It is just on the wrong pages.

The Question That Decides Everything Else
How much of your work is leadwork, zinc and sheet metal, as against general replacement and repair? ⭐ If the answer is “most of it”, you are competing with very few firms and the whole plan is about being found by the people who specify that work. If the answer is “a bit of it”, you are competing on price with every roofer in Essex and the plan is a different one. We would rather establish that in the first five minutes than guess at it.
